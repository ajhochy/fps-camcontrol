covered by this repository's MIT license. No third-party tracking library,
face recognizer, persistent embedding store, OpenCV or video-codec framework is
used. Synthetic fixtures are generated by these tests.

ONNX Runtime1.23.2 (MIT), NumPy2.5.3 (BSD and bundled permissive notices),
Pillow12.3.0 (MIT-CMU and bundled codec notices), websockets17.1 (BSD-3-Clause),
coloredlogs15.0.1 and humanfriendly10.0 (MIT), flatbuffers25.12.19 (Apache-2.0),
packaging26.3 (Apache-2.0 OR BSD-2-Clause), protobuf7.36.2 (BSD-3-Clause),
SymPy1.14.0 and mpmath1.3.0 (BSD).

The selected NumPy **macosx_14_0_arm64** wheel uses system Accelerate. Do not
substitute its macosx_11_0 wheel, which includes a different compiler/BLAS native
payload. Tracking requires macOS14; manual CamControl remains separately built.

Shipping must retain every wheel's exact `.dist-info` licenses and ONNX Runtime
`ThirdPartyNotices.txt`, plus separately recovered exact-version coloredlogs,
humanfriendly and flatbuffers license texts. Pillow's full native-codec notices
and the FreeType FTL option must be retained. The Python3.12.14 standalone
runtime's install-only archive omits full component notices; package the matching
full-build `python/licenses/` and `PYTHON.json` manifest. The checked manifest,
native library audit and notice-source digests are recorded in
`docs/ai/runs/2026-10-01-tracking-implementation-handoff.md`.

These attribution notes do not replace the exact component license files in
the final distribution. Sony SDK/CameraWebApp is user-supplied and is not bundled.
