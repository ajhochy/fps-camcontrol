No controller
  1. Pair the controller in Settings › Bluetooth on this iPad (hold its pairing button until the light blinks).
  2. Come back here and press any button on it. The status above names it when the page sees it.
  3. Hold Menu for 1 second to take control; the sticks and buttons then work like the desk controller.

While a stick or button is being touched the controller wins over the on-screen joystick. Letting the iPad sleep or switching away hands control back.
